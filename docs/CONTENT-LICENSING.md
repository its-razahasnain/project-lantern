# Content licensing workflow

No movie, episode, dubbed audio track, subtitle file, poster, or trailer may be published unless its rights status is documented.

For each asset/title, verify:
- Identity of the rights holder and chain of title.
- Countries/territories.
- Start and end dates.
- AVOD and advertising permissions.
- Web, mobile, and desktop rights.
- Download/offline rights.
- Device-to-device sharing rights.
- Dubbed audio and subtitle rights by language.
- Promotional artwork/trailer permissions.
- Exclusivity, restrictions, takedown, and termination terms.

The admin workflow must default to **unpublished**. Missing, expired, or conflicting rights block publication.

This document is an operational checklist, not legal advice. Obtain local legal advice for Pakistan and each target market.
